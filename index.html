About Us

AI is moving faster
than people can follow.

The gap between those who understand AI and those who don't is growing every day. HumanitAI exists to close that gap — through education, not fear.

We believe everyone deserves to understand the technology shaping their lives. Not just engineers. Not just tech companies. Everyone.

As a Community Interest Company, all profits are reinvested into free and subsidised AI safety programmes for underserved communities across the UK.

AI safety is not a technical problem. It's a human one.
What We Do

Practical AI safety
education for everyone.

No jargon. No hype. Real understanding of how AI works, where it fails, and how to use it safely.

🎓

AI Safety Education

Workshops, courses, and resources that teach people how AI works, where it fails, and how to use it safely. Designed for all ages and backgrounds.

🛡️

Risk Awareness

Helping individuals and organisations identify AI risks before they become problems. Data privacy, bias, hallucination, and misuse — explained clearly.

🤝

Community Programmes

Free and subsidised AI literacy programmes for underserved communities, schools, and grassroots organisations across the UK.

📊

Research & Advocacy

Evidence-based research on AI's impact on society. Policy recommendations. Public awareness campaigns. Making AI safety a public conversation.

What We Do

Practical AI safety education that makes complex technology understandable for everyone.

🎓

AI Safety Education

Workshops, courses, and resources that teach people how AI works, where it fails, and how to use it safely. No jargon. No hype. Real understanding.

🛡️

Risk Awareness

Helping individuals and organisations identify AI risks before they become problems. Data privacy, bias, hallucination, and misuse — explained clearly.

🤝

Community Programmes

Free and subsidised AI literacy programmes for underserved communities, schools, and grassroots organisations across the UK.

📊

Research & Advocacy

Evidence-based research on AI's impact on society. Policy recommendations. Public awareness campaigns. Making AI safety a public conversation.

Why HumanitAI

AI is moving faster than most people can follow. The gap between those who understand it and those who don't is growing. HumanitAI exists to close that gap — through education, not fear. We believe everyone deserves to understand the technology shaping their lives.
